# Governance

Stop the workstream if implementation requires:

- independently parsing, decompressing, or decrypting REC content;
- modifying or distributing Polar SDK source;
- sending a secret through argv, environment variables, logs, manifests,
  filenames, summaries, or output;
- accepting an unverified SDK strategy or protected fixture;
- uploading SDK-derived, secret, or private material to public infrastructure.

Resume only after the SDK strategy, fixture authority, private handling, and
evidence review are approved under SPEC-005.
